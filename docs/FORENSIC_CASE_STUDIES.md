# Real-World Forensic Case Studies: DNA Phenotyping in Criminal Investigations

This document compiles real-world, peer-reviewed forensic casework where **DNA Phenotyping and Biogeographical Ancestry Analysis** directly contributed to solving major criminal investigations or exonerating wrongly suspected individuals.

---

## Case 1: The Murder of Eva Blanco (Spain, 1997 – Solved 2015)
### *Ancestry Prediction Cracked an 18-Year Cold Case*

| Parameter | Casework Data |
|---|---|
| **Victim & Crime** | Eva Blanco Puig (16 years old), abducted, sexually assaulted, and stabbed 30 times in Algete (Madrid) on April 20, 1997. |
| **Perpetrator** | **Ahmed Chelh** (46 years old at arrest). |
| **Forensic Evidence** | Semen stains recovered from the victim’s clothing (preserved since 1997). |
| **Investigating Lab** | **Prof. Ángel Carracedo** (Institute of Forensic Sciences, University of Santiago de Compostela, Spain). |
| **Phenotyping Technology** | Autosomal Ancestry-Informative Markers (AIM-SNPs) + Y-chromosome haplotype analysis. |
| **Phenotyping Predictions** | 1. **Biogeographical Origin:** The suspect was of **North African / Maghrebi origin** (Moroccan/Algerian descent), not native Spanish/European.<br>2. **Physical Pigmentation:** Brown eyes, dark hair, olive/medium-dark skin. |
| **Investigative Impact** | • For 17 years, Spanish Civil Guard investigated local Spanish youths without success.<br>• Carracedo's genetic profile completely overturned the police theory.<br>• Police cross-referenced the ~200 North African families who resided in Algete in 1997.<br>• A familial DNA match pointed to a brother whose DNA was in the database, leading directly to Ahmed Chelh in France. |
| **Final Confirmation & Outcome** | Arrested in Pierrefitte-sur-Seine (France) in October 2015. Confirmatory STR matching gave a random match probability of **$1 \times 10^{-18}$** (one in a quintillion). Convicted and sentenced. |

## Case 2: The Murder of Marianne Vaatstra (Netherlands, 1999 – Solved 2012)
### *Phenotyping Exonerated Wrongly Accused Groups*

| Parameter | Casework Data |
|---|---|
| **Victim & Crime** | Marianne Vaatstra (16 years old), murdered in Kollumerland, Friesland, on May 1, 1999. |
| **Perpetrator** | **Jasper Steringa** (45 years old at arrest, local dairy farmer). |
| **Forensic Evidence** | Semen recovered from the crime scene. |
| **Investigating Lab** | **Prof. Peter de Knijff** (Leiden University Medical Center) and **Prof. Manfred Kayser** (Erasmus MC Rotterdam — co-creator of HIrisPlex). |
| **Phenotyping Technology** | Y-STR profiling and Autosomal Pigmentation SNPs (precursor to IrisPlex/HIrisPlex). |
| **Phenotyping Predictions** | 1. **Ancestry:** 100% Western European / indigenous Frisian-Dutch descent.<br>2. **Phenotype:** Light eyes and light/medium hair. |
| **Investigative Impact** | • Following the murder, riots and intense anti-immigrant protests targeted a nearby asylum seekers' centre.<br>• The DNA phenotyping results **scientifically excluded the asylum seekers**, proving the killer was a local native Frisian man.<br>• This led Dutch authorities to launch the largest voluntary mass familial DNA dragnet in Dutch history (6,600 local men). |
| **Final Confirmation & Outcome** | In November 2012, Jasper Steringa submitted a voluntary swab. His DNA was a **100% STR match**. He confessed in December 2012 and was sentenced to 18 years in prison. |

## Case 3: Christy Mirack (Pennsylvania, USA, 1992 – Solved 2018)
### *The "Virtually Identical" Snapshot Composite*

| Parameter | Casework Data |
|---|---|
| **Victim & Crime** | Christy Mirack (25-year-old elementary school teacher), raped and strangled in her Lancaster apartment on December 21, 1992. |
| **Perpetrator** | **Raymond Charles Rowe** (known locally as "DJ Freez"). |
| **Forensic Evidence** | Semen extracted from the carpet and victim's body. |
| **Phenotyping Technology** | **Parabon NanoLabs Snapshot DNA Phenotyping** (developed under U.S. DoD / DTRA funding). |
| **Phenotyping Predictions** | 1. **Ancestry:** Predominantly European (Southern/Central European admixture).<br>2. **Eye Color:** Brown/Hazel.<br>3. **Hair Color:** Brown/Dark.<br>4. **Skin Tone:** Olive / Medium.<br>5. **Age Progression:** Digital composite aged to 25 and 50 years. |
| **Investigative Impact** | • In November 2017, the Lancaster County District Attorney released the Snapshot composite publicly.<br>• CeCe Moore used the underlying SNP microarray profile on GEDmatch to identify distant familial links, pointing to Raymond Rowe.<br>• When detectives compared Rowe's photograph to the Snapshot composite, Lancaster County District Attorney Craig Stedman remarked that the likeness was **"eerie and virtually identical."** |
| **Final Confirmation & Outcome** | Detectives covertly collected Rowe's discarded water bottle and chewing gum at a school event in May 2018. Confirmatory STR profile matched the crime scene. Rowe pleaded guilty in January 2019 and received life without parole. |

## Case 4: April Tinsley (Indiana, USA, 1988 – Solved 2018)
### *A 30-Year Cold Case Solved*

| Parameter | Casework Data |
|---|---|
| **Victim & Crime** | April Tinsley (8 years old), abducted and murdered in Fort Wayne, Indiana, in April 1988. The killer taunted police with notes left on barns and bicycles for 16 years. |
| **Perpetrator** | **John D. Miller** (59 years old at arrest). |
| **Forensic Evidence** | Semen stains recovered from the victim’s coat (1988) and from plastic bags left with taunting notes in 2004. |
| **Phenotyping Technology** | **Parabon NanoLabs Snapshot**. |
| **Phenotyping Predictions** | 1. **Ancestry:** 100% Northern/Central European.<br>2. **Eye Color:** Blue/Hazel.<br>3. **Hair Color:** Light brown to blond.<br>4. **Skin Tone:** Fair/Pale with freckling.<br>5. **Age-Progression:** Rendered at age 50. |
| **Investigative Impact** | • The Snapshot composite was released to the public in 2015, narrowing the suspect demographic drastically.<br>• In 2018, investigative genetic genealogy (IGG) produced two brothers as candidates: John D. Miller and his brother.<br>• John Miller matched the Snapshot composite in physical age, hair type, and skin pigmentation. |
| **Final Confirmation & Outcome** | In July 2018, detectives retrieved discarded used condoms from Miller’s curbside trash. The STR profile matched the 1988 evidence with a probability of **1 in 2.3 quintillion**. Miller immediately confessed upon interrogation, pleaded guilty, and was sentenced to 80 years. |

## Case 5: The Golden State Killer (California, USA, 1974–1986 – Solved 2018)
### *Narrowing a 40-Year Manhunt*

| Parameter | Casework Data |
|---|---|
| **Victim & Crime** | 13 murders, 51 rapes, and 120+ burglaries across California between 1974 and 1986 (East Area Rapist / Original Night Stalker). |
| **Perpetrator** | **Joseph James DeAngelo** (72 years old at arrest, former police officer). |
| **Forensic Evidence** | Semen evidence preserved from the 1980 Lyman and Charlene Smith double homicide (Ventura County). |
| **Phenotyping Technology** | **Parabon Snapshot** (commissioned by FBI investigator Paul Holes in 2017). |
| **Phenotyping Predictions** | 1. **Biogeographical Ancestry:** 100% Northern/Western European.<br>2. **Eye Color:** Blue/Green (high probability: ~85%).<br>3. **Hair Color:** Blond to light brown.<br>4. **Skin Tone:** Very fair, high likelihood of sun freckling. |
| **Investigative Impact** | • Over four decades, thousands of tips and conflicting witness descriptions confused the task force.<br>• The phenotyping analysis established that the killer had **zero non-European admixture**, ruling out hundreds of false suspect theories.<br>• The SNP data was subsequently uploaded to GEDmatch by Barbara Rae-Venter, building a family tree back to DeAngelo’s ancestors. |
| **Final Confirmation & Outcome** | Detectives surveilled DeAngelo and seized a discarded tissue from his car trash bin in April 2018. The STR match was conclusive. DeAngelo pleaded guilty to 13 counts of first-degree murder in June 2020 and received 11 consecutive life terms. |

## Case 6: Sierra Bouzigard (Louisiana, USA, 2009 – Released 2015)
### *The First Official Public DNA Phenotyping Release in History*

| Parameter | Casework Data |
|---|---|
| **Victim & Crime** | Sierra Bouzigard (19 years old), found murdered near Lake Charles, Louisiana, on November 23, 2009. |
| **Forensic Evidence** | Foreign DNA extracted from under the victim's fingernails (defensive scrapings). |
| **Phenotyping Technology** | **Parabon Snapshot** (Released publicly in April 2015). |
| **The Historic Significance** | • **Initial eyewitness statements claimed the suspect was a Black male or dark-skinned local.**<br>• Police spent five years following dead-end leads focusing on local Black suspects.<br>• In 2015, Parabon’s phenotyping revealed the suspect had:
  * **~75% Northern European / ~25% Hispanic/Native American ancestry**
  * **Fair/medium skin, brown hair, hazel/green eyes**<br>• **Calcasieu Parish Sheriff’s Office officially released the Snapshot composite to the public** — the **first time in forensic history** that a DNA composite was used in an active murder investigation.<br>• It stopped wrongful targeting of the wrong demographic community. |
